# TRAINER NOTES — CONTINGENCY
## When the synchronous morning cannot run at all

---

> **Read this only if the live morning session is lost** — a typhoon, a power cut, a platform
> outage, a cohort that cannot get online. The standard Day 1 is in the **Trainer Guide**:
> online-synchronous 8:00–12:00, fully asynchronous 1:00–5:00. This document covers the day
> where even the morning has to go.

---

## What you lose, and it is not recoverable by document

| Unit | Element | Without a live session |
|------|---------|-----------------------|
| **400311101** | **LO1** — Follow routine **spoken** messages | ❌ **Cannot be delivered.** The evidence guide requires the candidate to receive verbal instructions, record them, and seek clarification. An assessor must observe it live |
| **400311102** | LO1, LO2 — Work with others | ❌ **Cannot be delivered.** Requires observed participation in a work group |
| **400311107** | (induction) | ⚠️ **Partly.** They can audit their own workstation; the lab checklist waits |
| **400311108** | (induction) | ❌ **No.** The Green Lab Pledge applies to the training lab |
| **400311101** | **LO2** — Perform duties following **written** notices | ✅ **Yes.** Entirely document-based |

**So a fully asynchronous Day 1 delivers LO2 and nothing else. Do not record Day 1 as
complete.** You owe the cohort roughly two and a half hours of live delivery, and it has to
happen before the Day 3 assessment window.

---

## Reschedule the morning before you send anything out

Do this first, not last. The trainees need to know when the live block is happening, and
saying "we will find a time" on Day 1 is how it ends up on Day 6.

**The three blocks you must reconvene, in priority order:**

| Block | Minutes | Why it cannot wait |
|-------|---------|-------------------|
| Follow routine spoken messages — handover, read-back, clarifying questions | 65 | The whole of LO1. Nothing substitutes for it |
| Work with others — team formation and the shift huddle | 35 | 400311102 LO2 requires observed contribution |
| OSH induction and the lab-rules briefing | 30 | Sets the scanning boundary before Day 11 |

**Where to put them:** the front of the next live session. If Day 2 is live, run them
8:00–10:15 on Day 2 and compress the Day 2 fault-finding block — Day 2 has more slack in it
than Day 1 does. Do not defer past Day 3.

---

## What you send out

Send the **Self-Study Pack** with the covering message below, plus:

| File | Note |
|------|------|
| `SOP_Pack` | Needed for Tasks 5 and 6, and every day after |
| `Student_Handout` | Task 4 |
| `Home_Workstation_Audit` | Task 6 substitute — see the amendments below |
| The pre-test link | Task 1 |

### Amendments the trainees need in writing

The Self-Study Pack assumes a live morning happened. Tell them what changes:

- **Task 3 (shift handover write-up) is deferred.** There is no spoken handover to write up
  yet. They do it on the day you reconvene, live, from notes they take on the day.
- **The Home Workstation Audit moves into the pack** — they complete and sign it on their own
  instead of on the call. Ask for a photo of the desk alongside the sheet. It is not
  observation, but it is a great deal better than a sheet of twenty passes.
- **Task 8 gains a question:** *"One security thing that has happened to you or someone you
  know, and how you found out."* That is the icebreaker, collected in writing. It is the one
  genuine consolation of losing the morning — you arrive at the live session already knowing
  their stories and able to group them by theme.
- **The Huddle Observation Checklist is not required** — there is no huddle to observe yet.

**Revised total: about 3 hours 30 minutes.**

>

> does **not** mean it is a day off.

> your own pace. Please also open the documents it refers to: the **SOP Pack**, the **Day 1
> Student Handout**, the **Home Workstation Audit**, and this link for the **pre-test**: [LINK]
>
> **Three changes to the pack, because there was no session this morning:**
>
> 1. **Skip Task 3.** It asks you to write up a spoken handover you have not heard yet. We do
>    it live on [DATE].
> 2. **Do the Home Workstation Audit on your own**, and send a photo of your desk with it.
> 3. **Add one answer to Task 8:** one security thing that has happened to you or someone you
>    know — and, specifically, *how you found out*. That last part matters more than it sounds.
>
> **Three things to know before you start:**
>
> 1. **The pre-test is not an exam.** It does not affect your result. Please do not look
>    anything up — an honest low score tells me where to spend the next fifteen days, and a

> 2. **Everything else in the pack is real portfolio evidence.** Keep it. We will not repeat it
>    in class.
> 3. **Do not switch tabs during the pre-test.** It is monitored, and leaving the test costs
>    you time.
>
> **The parts of Day 1 that need all of us together — receiving a spoken handover, reading
> instructions back, running a shift huddle — happen on [DATE] at [TIME].** Put it in your

>
> Any problems at all, message me.

## When you reconvene — the running order

You have roughly **two hours ten** of owed delivery. Everything else in the standard morning
is already covered by the pack they completed.

| Time | Session | Note |
|------|---------|------|
| 0:00–0:15 | Welcome, collect the pack, confirm who is missing what | Shortened — orientation was in the pack |
| 0:15–0:35 | Icebreaker, using their submitted incident stories | Shortened, and better — you can group by theme |
| 0:35–1:05 | OSH and environmental induction, lab rules, the scanning boundary | As per the Trainer Guide, 9:15 block |
| 1:05–1:20 | Team formation and breakout assignment | Must precede the drills |
| 1:20–2:25 | **Follow routine spoken messages — the full 65-minute block** | **Unabridged.** This is the whole reason you reconvened |
| 2:25–2:35 | Break | |
| 2:35–3:10 | **Work with others — RACI in three minutes, then two rounds of huddle** | Two rounds, so every trainee leads once |
| 3:10–3:40 | **Debrief the SOP markup and the notice triage** | Go straight to Task 5 Q1 and Task 6 question C |
| 3:40–3:55 | Shift handover write-up — Task 3, completed live from the morning briefing | Collect it before they leave |

> **Spend the recovered time on the read-back drill and the huddle.** They are the things that
> need everyone present, they are what an assessor will want to see, and they are always the
> first casualties when a day overruns.
