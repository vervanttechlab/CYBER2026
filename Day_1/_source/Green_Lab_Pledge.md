# GREEN LAB PLEDGE
## Commitment to Environmentally Responsible Computing

---

I, **_________________________**, as a trainee in the Cyber Threat Monitoring Level I program, pledge to practise the following environmentally responsible habits in this lab and in my future workplace.

---

### Digital-First Practices
- [ ] I will keep evidence and worksheets digitally wherever the assessor permits it
- [ ] I will share files through the shared folder or a USB drive rather than on paper
- [ ] I will take notes digitally when it is practical to do so
- [ ] I will not print screenshots that can be stored as image files

### Energy Conservation
- [ ] I will switch off my monitor during lunch and long breaks
- [ ] I will shut my computer down properly at the end of each day
- [ ] I will not leave a machine running overnight unless the trainer has asked me to
- [ ] I will use sleep mode when I step away briefly
- [ ] I will not leave a virtual machine or a scan running when I leave the room

> **Why this matters in a security lab specifically:** monitoring tools and scans are designed to run continuously. A vulnerability scan left running overnight consumes power, generates heat, and produces log data that nobody reads. Stopping what you started is both good practice and good discipline.

### Responsible Printing
- [ ] I will print double-sided whenever possible
- [ ] I will preview before printing so I do not waste pages
- [ ] I will use draft quality for anything that is not a final document
- [ ] I will reuse single-sided paper as scratch paper for drills

### E-Waste Awareness
- [ ] I will not put batteries, cables, or electronic components in the general waste bin
- [ ] I will report damaged equipment to the lab administrator rather than discarding it
- [ ] I will handle hardware carefully so that it lasts longer
- [ ] I understand that storage media may hold sensitive data and must be **wiped or destroyed properly**, not simply thrown away

> **Why this matters in a security lab specifically:** a discarded hard drive or USB stick is a data breach waiting to happen. Secure disposal of storage media is both an environmental practice and a security control. The two are the same action.

### Resource Conservation
- [ ] I will switch off lights and air conditioning when the room is empty
- [ ] I will report water leaks or dripping taps in the lab area
- [ ] I will keep my workstation and the lab clean and organised
